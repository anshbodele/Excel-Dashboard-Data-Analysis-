# Excel Sales Dashboard

## Problem Statement
A multinational retail company operates across 5 regions (Africa, Asia-Pacific, Europe, North America, South America) selling 5 product lines (Clothing, Electronics, Furniture, Healthcare, Software).

The management team lacks a centralized view of:

Which product lines generate the highest revenue and profit

Which regions and departments drive performance

How payment methods and expenses impact overall profitability

Without a clear dashboard, decision-makers struggle to identify growth opportunities, optimize costs, and allocate resources effectively.

**Goal**: Build an interactive Excel dashboard that provides clear visibility into sales performance, profit drivers, and operational efficiency to support data-driven decision-making.

## Key Insights
- **Best Product Line**: Healthcare ($21.8M revenue, $705.9K profit)
- **Best Payment Method**: Cash ($9M profit)
- **Top Region (Volume)**: Africa (818 transactions)
- **Top Department**: R&D ($8.3M profit)

## Dashboard Features
- Profit breakdown by Product Line and Region
- Revenue vs Expenses by Department
- Transaction volume by Region
- Interactive Slicers for filtering
